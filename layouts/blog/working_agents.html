01 / Foundation

Two dimensions.
Different questions.

Persistence describes how long responsibility stays open. Openness describes how much of the work world must be established during execution.

↗
TEMPORAL RESPONSIBILITY

Work persistence

From immediate closure to responsibilities that remain active across feedback, stages, external events, and time.

Immediate closure⟶Standing obligation
↔
THE TASK WORLD

Work-world openness

From enumerated materials and interfaces to a workspace whose objects, relations, authority, and actors must be discovered while working.

Predefined task world⟶Open workspace

These axes describe working conditions. They are independent of intelligence and autonomy.

04 / Optimization

Experience becomes
system capability.

Diagnose the failure before choosing what to update. The right intervention can reside in model parameters, external support, or their coupling.

Work execution→Observable experience→Diagnosis & feedback→System update ↺
MMODEL OPTIMIZATION

Learn the
recurring capability.

Internalize reusable interpretation and decision patterns in model parameters.

  • Mid-training & supervised fine-tuning
  • Agentic reinforcement learning
  • On-policy distillation
  • Self-improving training
HHARNESS OPTIMIZATION

Improve the
external support.

Repair what the model can observe, invoke, remember, and verify during execution.

  • Interfaces & tools
  • Prompts & workflows
  • Memory & reusable skills
  • Execution control & feedback
M + HCOUPLING & JOINT OPTIMIZATION

Adapt the
working pair.

Account for the interaction between learned behavior and the harness supporting it.

  • Harness-aware model training
  • Learned harness design
  • Joint model–harness optimization
  • Matched comparisons & retention

05 / Evaluation

What does the
evidence establish?

A requested outcome and the properties an evaluator checks are different objects. Capability claims must stay within what the evidence can support.

→
02Observed evidenceAnswer, state, artifact, trace

03Judging procedureRules, rubrics, models, experts

04Reported metricSuccess, quality, reliability, cost
→
05Capability claimBounded by inspected properties
A

Answer

Does the response satisfy the question and agree with its supporting evidence?

Reference answers · Source support
S

State

Did the intended effect actually happen, with relevant constraints and other state preserved?

Tests · Database checks · Effects
O

Artifact

Is the native deliverable correct, editable, source-consistent, and usable in its intended form?

Native objects · Rendered views
W

Workflow

Were dependencies, prerequisites, approvals, and intermediate obligations respected?

Checkpoints · Traces · Handoffs
A workbook example

A task can request a correct, editable, source-consistent workbook while a verifier checks selected cells. Those checks establish correctness over the inspected cells; editability, provenance, and other structure need corresponding evidence.
