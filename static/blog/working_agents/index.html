Abstract

As foundation models evolve from information generation to digital work execution, agents are increasingly capable of completing complex tasks across tools, applications, and workflows. However, existing research lacks a unified perspective on how agents reliably accomplish delegated work. To address this gap, we present a work-centric, system-level perspective that views work agents as systems combining foundation models and runtime harnesses to execute tasks in stateful workspaces and deliver verifiable outcomes. We introduce two complementary dimensions, work persistence and work openness, to characterize working conditions, and classify digital work into five forms according to task scope and organization. Building on this framework, we identify five core capability families required for reliable work execution and examine approaches to model optimization, harness optimization, and their joint optimization. We further analyze existing evaluation methods and metrics from the perspective of work outcomes. Finally, we discuss future research directions, including sustained work delegation, adaptation to open environments, and the interaction between work execution and capability improvement. This paper provides a unified framework for understanding, optimizing, and evaluating work agents, supporting the development of systems capable of delivering reliable, verifiable, and practically valuable work outcomes.

02 / Work forms

What work do
agents undertake?

Five forms describe the scope and organization of delegated work. Scope concerns objectives, stages, objects, and dependencies.

Conversation Level

Produce an informational outcome.

Interpret a request and its associated evidence to produce an answer, analysis, classification, judgment, or specification. Completion is assessed on the returned information, without requiring a change to source artifacts or external state.

Atomic Action Level

One local objective, a concrete effect.

Apply a local operation to a clearly defined work object and confirm its intended effect. The scope of the objective defines this form; several mouse, keyboard, or API operations may still be needed.

A WORK SCENARIO

Multi-step Level

Coordinate actions toward a complete result.

Coordinate dependent actions toward a complete objective. Intermediate results supply evidence or state for later decisions, and execution feedback can change the remaining sequence. Completion is assessed on the final result.

Workflow Level

Keep stages, rules, and handoffs aligned.

Advance a delegated operational process through stages, procedural requirements, approvals, and interactions with other participants. Completion covers the process outcome as well as the final output.

Establish which files, records, and applications bear on the goal and how they are related. Locate relevant objects, reconcile evidence and versions, and preserve consistency among affected objects when updates are required.

03 / Capabilities

What makes
work reliable?

Five capability families form a recurring cycle around shared work context. Evidence guides decisions; verification can send work back to the capability where a problem arises. The running example updates a workbook, report, charts, and slides.

01

Perception & understanding

Interpret objects and relationships.

Read native work objects, identify relevant evidence, and trace sources, versions, and dependencies across the workspace.

Evidence · Objects · Relationships
02

Reasoning & decision making

Make grounded decisions.

Reconcile evidence with goals, professional knowledge, business rules, and constraints before choosing an action.

Evidence · Constraints · Judgment
03

Planning & coordination

Organize dependent work.

Decompose objectives, track prerequisites, coordinate stages and participants, and manage branches and handoffs.

Goals · Dependencies · Handoffs
04

Execution & state management

Turn intent into effects.

Act on the workspace, distinguish attempted actions from committed changes, and preserve consistency across objects and applications.

Actions · Effects · Shared state
05

Verification, recovery & replanning

Establish and repair the result.

Check outcomes against requirements, investigate uncertain effects, and recover or replan when assumptions fail.

Checks · Diagnosis · Replanning

06 / The path forward

Beyond bounded tasks.
Toward dependable work.

Personal work agents, human digital twins, and organizational work systems make sustained delegation concrete. They motivate advances in environment, persistence, and verification.

CONTINUING SERVICE

Personal work agents

Carry a user’s goals across requests; advance unfinished work and choose when their attention is needed.

PERSONAL JUDGMENT

Human digital twins

Represent a particular person’s judgment, with separate checks for professional quality and personal fidelity.

COORDINATION ACROSS ROLES

Organizational work systems

Connect contributions, decision rights, and dependencies across people and agents.

01
Environment

Generalization needs realistic work.

Construct work environments that preserve realistic objects, relationships, histories, and professional requirements. Study transfer across work settings and differences between people.

Held-out transfer · Source selection · Realistic curricula
↗
02
Persistence

Responsibility outlasts one execution.

Retain goals, judgments, commitments, and dependencies across events and interruptions. Choose when to observe, ask, act, or wait, and adapt procedures without losing track of current work.

Fulfilled obligations · Duplicate effects · Continuity
